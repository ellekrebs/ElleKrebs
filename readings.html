Plato's Cave

I use photography and image making to express my perspective on the world. Moments and scenes and how they are captured on camera, their perspective, is the unique artisanship of photography. Likewise, image making, the intentional collaboration of visuals, allows us as artists to express our perspectives. The directive nature of the medium allows me to express myself with the visuals that surround me. Though we are in an age where everyone has a camera in their pocket, as artists, direction and perspective is the artistic distinction. When images are captured, intention is what is important. Intention is what sets me apart from Aunt Sally taking a group photo at the family function, though it is arguable that she would be an artist too. I digress as “what makes an image art?” or “what is art?” is another (complicated) ball of wax. The question at hand is how do I visually represent the world. I do this by translating my influences into my work. Everything we are exposed to influences us because the world is what we make sense of as artists, so everything my senses absorb influences the way I make images through any medium.

Photography, image making, or the combination of image and text sponsor my ability as an artist to express myself and my perspective on the notions of our world today. In a world that is so complex and out of our individual control, image making of any sort is the avenue for making sense of the world’s complexities. Personally I like to work with mixed media projects especially physically (not digital). I like working with physical prints and materials to play with visuals in hand. I like the personal and unique expression of this practice and the result of the work. I find working by hand to be the best way for me to express my perspectives of the world, my vision. It feels human to me and that perspective is what is reflected in my creations. Even the artistic preference to work with paper and the reasons for which is also an artistic choice that reflects perspective and influence. So, it is impossible to truly put into words how I or anyone exactly translates their worldview into the images that they make. However, for me I can say that exposure to other artists’ work in any medium (not just imaging) can inspire new perspectives within me as an artist.

As we are in the digital age, the world is evermore difficult to navigate. The artistic benefit of this is that now more than ever there is opportunity to create and more complex mediums to create with. While I do find it daunting to explore creating digitally sometimes (most of my technical photography experience is manual), I do believe it is important to remain with the times and progress with technology, even as an artist, not that there is not a strong statement in not doing as I mentioned before. Thus, regardless of the medium, I think art is the best way to express our world views.
