Elle Krebs

Creative Statement

As a media maker, I create to share my perspectives and worldview. I am inspired even by the garbage I walk by on the street, and I am an artist of many mediums. I have been dancing in ballet since I could walk and now I am an aspiring theatrical singer dancer. I have been working to become a well-rounded and fulfilled artist all my life not just in the performing arts, but in visual art as well. I picked up black and white film photography in high school. I absolutely fell in love, immersing myself in photohistory and most importantly the darkroom. There was a substantial time in my life where I reeked of hairspray and fixative at all times. That time was when I truly made up my mind that I wanted to pursue art seriously.
Now I am working on both a Media and Music major at Hunter College while working at the Hudson Theatre on Broadway as an usher. I am a very lucky artist, everyday surrounded by art and influences. In this chapter of my artistic journey, I am exploring and thinking about what I want to create and how I want to make it happen. The world is a crazy place, and I am still in the early stages of deciphering it. This course is an opportunity for me to reflect on my artistic intentions as well as expanding my knowledge and craft technically.
